Animal welfare

How we care for our cats

Every cat at Whiskers Café is vet-checked, microchipped, and given a quiet space to retreat to whenever they need a break from visitors.

Rest first

Cats always have access to private, visitor-free rooms and are never forced to interact.

Regular vet care

All cats are vaccinated, neutered, and receive routine check-ups from our partner vet practice.

Adoption support

We work with local shelters to home-check every adoption and offer support after cats leave us.

Visitor rules

Before you visit, please read

  • 🖐️Let the cat approach you first — never chase or pick a cat up without an introduction.
  • 🍽️Please don't feed the cats human food, even as a treat — it can make them unwell.
  • 📷Photos are welcome, but please turn off camera flash.
  • 🚪Doors to the lounge must be closed gently and fully behind you at all times.
  • 🧴Please use the hand sanitiser provided before and after handling any cat.
  • 👶Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult in the lounge at all times.
  • 😌If a cat walks away or hides, that's their choice — please let them be.
